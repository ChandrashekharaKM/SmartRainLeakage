Physical Leak Indicators

  • Continuous Non-Zero Night Flow: Normal households display zero or near-zero flow between 1:00 AM and 5:00 AM. Constant flow during this period strongly signifies a leak.
  • Pressure Drop Correlation: When an abnormal leak develops, pipeline pressure frequently dips below nominal levels (approx. 3.5 bar down to ~3.2 bar).
  • Flow Duration: Normal tap uses last between 1-15 minutes/hour. Leaks run uninterrupted for 55-60 minutes/hour.

Statistical Anomaly Engine

The regression residual is defined as:
residual = actual - predicted

Heteroscedastic noise model:
σ = (c0 + c1 × predicted) × 1.2533

Z = residual / σ
